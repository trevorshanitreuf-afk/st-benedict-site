About the school

St. Benedict Secondary School is in Buwama, Mpigi District, Uganda. It appears in the Ministry of Education’s list of private schools (March 2022) and in public school directories.

Published examination tables show that the school has taken part in both UCE (O-Level) and UACE (A-Level).

Message from the Headteacher

Mr Wasswa Ssemattimba Nicholas

Welcome to St. Benedict Secondary School, Buwama.

It is my great pleasure to welcome you to our school and to our digital home. St. Benedict Secondary School is committed to creating an environment in which young people are encouraged to learn, grow, discover their abilities and prepare themselves for the opportunities and responsibilities of the future.

We believe that education goes beyond the classroom. It is about developing knowledge, discipline, integrity, confidence, respect for others and a sense of responsibility towards society. Through the dedication of our teachers, the commitment of our learners and the support of parents and our community, we continue striving to nurture individuals who are prepared to face the challenges of an ever-changing world.

Our school journey is built on the belief that every learner has potential worth developing. We encourage curiosity, hard work, creativity, good conduct and a spirit of cooperation, while providing opportunities for learners to grow academically, socially and personally.

This website provides a window into our school — our learning environment, activities, achievements, community and aspirations. We invite prospective learners, parents, former students and friends of the school to explore our story and become part of our journey.

As we continue to pursue excellence in education and personal development, we remain committed to building a school community where every learner is encouraged to aspire, every effort is valued and every opportunity contributes to a brighter future.

Thank you for visiting St. Benedict Secondary School, Buwama. We look forward to sharing our journey with you.

Together, we learn. Together, we grow. Together, we shape the future.

The Headteacher
